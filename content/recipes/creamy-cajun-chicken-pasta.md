## Ingredients

- 2 boneless, skinless chicken breasts, diced
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 small yellow onion, diced
- 2 tsp Cajun seasoning
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- 1 cup heavy cream
- 8 oz penne or rigatoni pasta

## Directions

1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil and cook the pasta according to package instructions until al dente. Reserve 1/2 cup of pasta water before draining, then set the pasta aside.

2. While the pasta cooks, heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the diced chicken and season with the Cajun seasoning, salt, and pepper. Cook, stirring occasionally, until browned and cooked through, about 6–8 minutes. Transfer the chicken to a plate.

3. Reduce the heat to medium. In the same skillet, add the onion and cook until softened, about 3–4 minutes. Add the garlic and cook for another 30 seconds until fragrant.

4. Pour in the heavy cream and stir to combine, scraping up any browned bits from the bottom of the pan. Let the sauce simmer for 3–4 minutes until it begins to thicken slightly.

5. Return the chicken to the skillet, then add the cooked pasta and toss everything together until well coated. If the sauce is too thick, loosen it with a splash of the reserved pasta water. Taste and adjust seasoning before serving.
