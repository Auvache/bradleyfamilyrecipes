## Ingredients

- 2 tablespoons cornstarch
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 teaspoon dry mustard (optional)
- 1/4 teaspoon pepper
- 2 1/2 cups milk
- 2 tablespoons margarine or butter
- 2 cups (8 oz) shredded American or cheddar cheese, divided
- 8 ounces elbow macaroni (about 2 cups dry), cooked 5 minutes and drained

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 375°F.

2. In a medium saucepan, combine cornstarch, salt, dry mustard, and pepper. Stir in milk.

3. Add margarine. Stirring constantly, bring to a boil over medium-high heat and boil 1 minute.

4. Remove from heat. Stir in 1 3/4 cups cheese until melted.

5. Add the cooked elbows. Pour into a greased 2-quart casserole. Sprinkle with reserved cheese.

6. Bake uncovered 25 minutes or until lightly browned.
