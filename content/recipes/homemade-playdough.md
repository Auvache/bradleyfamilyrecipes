## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup boiling water
- 1/2 cup flour
- 1 teaspoon cream of tartar
- 1/4 cup salt
- 1 1/2 teaspoons canola oil
- 1 pack Kool-Aid or 4-5 drops of food coloring

## Directions

1. Combine water and food coloring in a pot and bring to a boil.

2. While waiting for the water to boil, combine your dry ingredients in a separate bowl.

3. Remove from heat and add the oil.

4. Slowly stir in the dry ingredients. Continue mixing until the playdough forms a ball.

5. When the playdough is cool enough, knead it until it is no longer sticky. Flour your hands first to prevent sticking.
