## Ingredients

- 2 lbs boneless skinless chicken breasts
- 1 block (8 oz) cream cheese
- 1 stick butter
- 2 cans cream of chicken soup
- 1 packet dry Italian seasoning mix

## Directions

1. Place all ingredients in the crockpot.

2. Cook on low for 4-5 hours.

3. Shred or slice the chicken and serve.
