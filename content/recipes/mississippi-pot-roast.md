## Ingredients

- 1 (3-4 lb) beef chuck roast
- 1 (1 oz) packet dry ranch dressing mix
- 1 (1 oz) packet au jus or brown gravy mix
- 1/2 cup (1 stick) unsalted butter
- 8-10 whole pepperoncini peppers
- 1/4 cup pepperoncini juice from the jar (optional)

## Directions

1. Place the chuck roast in the bottom of a 6 quart slow cooker. Browning it first is optional and not necessary.

2. Sprinkle the ranch packet and the au jus packet directly over the top of the roast. Do not stir.

3. Set the stick of butter on top of the roast, then scatter the pepperoncini peppers around it. Add the pepper juice if you want a little more tang.

4. Do not add any water or broth. The butter and the juices from the roast make all the liquid this needs.

5. Cover and cook on LOW for 8-10 hours or HIGH for 6-7 hours, without lifting the lid.

6. Shred the beef right in the slow cooker with two forks, discarding any large pieces of fat. Stir it through the buttery gravy and serve over mashed potatoes, egg noodles, or on rolls.
