## Ingredients

- 1 large onion, diced
- 4 boneless skinless chicken breasts
- 1 (10.5 oz) can condensed cream of chicken soup
- 1 (10.5 oz) can condensed cream of celery soup
- 1 tsp poultry seasoning
- 1 tbsp chopped fresh parsley
- Black pepper to taste
- 2 cups reduced sodium chicken broth
- 2 cups frozen peas and carrots, thawed
- 1 (16.3 oz) can refrigerated biscuits (buttermilk, country, or homestyle)

## Directions

1. Scatter the diced onion across the bottom of a 6 quart slow cooker and lay the chicken breasts on top.

2. Stir the cream of chicken soup, cream of celery soup, poultry seasoning, parsley, and pepper together in a bowl, then spread it over the chicken. Pour the broth over everything.

3. Cover and cook on HIGH for 4 hours.

4. Roll each biscuit thin and cut it into 4 strips. Stir the thawed peas and carrots into the slow cooker, then lay the biscuit strips over the top and push them halfway down into the gravy so they soak up liquid as they cook. Put the lid back on quickly.

5. Cook 1 more hour on HIGH, until the dumplings are cooked through and fluffy.

6. Lift out the chicken breasts, shred them lightly with two forks, and stir them back in. The dumplings will break apart some, which is exactly right. Cook another 10 minutes and serve.
